Latin Modern Math
=================

latinmodern-math.otf is the font rocketplot typesets math with (the text between $ signs in titles, labels,
legend names and annotations). It is compiled into the rocketplot library as an array of bytes.

Font:     Latin Modern Math
Authors:  Bogusław Jackowski, Piotr Strzelczyk and Piotr Pianowski
